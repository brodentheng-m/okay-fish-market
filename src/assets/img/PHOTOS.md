# Photos — needed from Dad

Plain notes on the photos the site needs. These replace the dashed
`DAD-PHOTO` placeholder blocks in `index.html` (the blocks are wired to the
slots below; swap the placeholder for the matching photo when you have it).

## Photo slots

1. **Fish counter hero shot**
   - What: the display counter full of ice and fresh fish, shop in the
     background, good light.
   - Size: 1920 × 1080 (16:9), landscape.
   - Format: JPEG or WebP. Keep the file under ~500 KB.

2. **Fish fillets & whole fish**
   - What: fillets on the counter plus some whole fish (snapper, flounder,
     whatever is fresh that day).
   - Size: 1600 × 1200 (4:3), landscape.
   - Format: JPEG or WebP.

3. **Shellfish — shrimp, lobster, clams**
   - What: shrimp, lobsters, clams (or any combo) on ice.
   - Size: 1600 × 1200 (4:3), landscape.
   - Format: JPEG or WebP.

4. **Prepared items — chowder, ceviche, daily specials**
   - What: a container of chowder and/or a bowl of ceviche, something the
     market actually makes.
   - Size: 1600 × 1200 (4:3), landscape.
   - Format: JPEG or WebP.

## Notes

- Use a phone camera, good natural light, no clutter. Square-ish crop of any
  of the above also works if that suits a slot better.
- Land photos as `jpg`/`webp` files in `src/assets/img/` and reference them
  from `index.html` where the `DAD-PHOTO` block sits.
- Keep files small (below ~500 KB each) — the site is meant to stay fast.
